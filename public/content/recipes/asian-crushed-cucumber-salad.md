## Ingredients

- 2 cucumbers
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- Sesame seeds, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Place the cucumbers on a cutting board and gently crush them using a meat mallet (meat tenderizer) until they split open.
2. Cut them into bite-sized pieces.
3. Sprinkle with a little salt and let them rest for 10–15 minutes. Drain off any excess liquid.
4. Mix together the garlic, soy sauce, rice vinegar, mirin, and sesame oil.
5. Pour the dressing over the cucumbers and toss well.
6. Finish with a sprinkle of sesame seeds and serve chilled.
