## Ingredients

- 5–6 chicken breasts
- 500 g cherry tomatoes
- 3/4 cup green olives
- 3/4 cup black olives
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 1 1/2 tsp salt, or to taste
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves (or 1 tsp dried thyme)

## Instructions

1. Arrange the chicken breasts in a baking tray. Drizzle with olive oil, then season with the salt, black pepper, lemon juice, and lemon zest.
2. Scatter the cherry tomatoes, green olives, and black olives around the chicken, then sprinkle with the thyme.
3. Bake in a preheated oven at 220°C for 20 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through and the tomatoes are soft and juicy.
4. Serve immediately.
