## Ingredients

- 1 kg beef fillet, cut into thin strips or bite-sized pieces
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 500 g mushrooms, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup white wine
- 400 ml cooking cream
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pan. Add the diced onion and sauté until soft and translucent.
2. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the beef fillet and cook over high heat until browned and cooked to your liking.
4. Pour in the white wine and let it simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until slightly reduced.
5. Meanwhile, in another pan, heat the remaining olive oil with the butter.
6. Add the mushrooms and sauté until golden and any excess liquid has evaporated.
7. Add the mushrooms to the beef mixture.
8. Season with salt and black pepper.
9. Stir in the cream and let everything simmer gently for 5 minutes until the sauce is smooth and creamy.
10. Adjust seasoning if needed.
11. Serve hot with steamed rice or pasta.
12. Garnish with a little chopped parsley if desired.
