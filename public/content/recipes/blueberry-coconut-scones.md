## Ingredients

- 2 cups (320 g) wholemeal self-raising flour, plus extra for dusting
- 65 g brown sugar
- 20 g cold unsalted butter, cut into small pieces
- 1/3 cup (65 g) dried blueberries
- 1/4 cup (20 g) shredded coconut
- 1 1/3 cups (330 ml) buttermilk
- 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
- 1 cup (160 g) frozen blueberries

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C and line a baking tray with parchment paper.
2. In a large bowl, combine the flour and brown sugar. Add the cold butter and rub it into the flour with your fingertips until you have a crumbly, sand-like texture.
3. Stir in the dried blueberries and shredded coconut.
4. Make a well in the centre and pour in the buttermilk and vanilla. Gently mix with a butter knife until the dough just starts to come together.
5. Add the frozen blueberries and fold them in gently. Don't overmix — the less you work the dough, the lighter your scones will be.
6. Turn the dough onto the prepared tray and, with lightly floured hands, gently shape it into a 20 cm round.
7. Cut into 8 wedges, keeping them together on the tray.
8. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until puffed and beautifully golden on top.
9. Let them cool slightly, then serve warm.
