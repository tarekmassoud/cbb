## Ingredients

- 500 g flour
- 2 tbsp salt
- 420 g lukewarm water
- 12 g instant yeast
- 1 tbsp olive oil, plus extra for the tray and drizzling
- 1 tsp honey
- Black olives (around 15)
- Sun-dried tomatoes (around 10)

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, combine the flour and salt.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the lukewarm water, instant yeast, olive oil, and honey.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the flour mixture and mix until a sticky dough forms.
4. Cover and let the dough rest for 10 minutes.
5. Perform one stretch-and-fold, then let the dough rest for another 10 minutes.
6. Repeat the stretch-and-fold once more.
7. Cover the dough and refrigerate for 24 to 48 hours.
8. Drizzle olive oil into a baking tray and line it with parchment paper. Lightly oil the parchment as well.
9. Transfer the dough to the prepared tray. Scatter the black olives and sun-dried tomatoes over the dough, then fold the dough over them to enclose the filling. Flip the dough over so the seam is underneath.
10. Drizzle the top with a little olive oil, cover the tray with another tray or plastic wrap, and let the dough rest for 2 hours.
11. Using well-oiled hands, gently stretch the dough to fill the tray, pressing your fingertips into the dough to create lots of bubbles and dimples.
12. Bake in a preheated 220°C oven for 20–25 minutes, or until golden brown.
13. Let it cool slightly, slice, and enjoy.
