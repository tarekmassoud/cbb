## Ingredients

- 1 whole cauliflower, cut into florets
- Olive oil
- Salt, to taste
- 5 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 bunch fresh coriander, chopped
- 1 tsp piment d'Espelette
- 1 lemon, for squeezing

## Instructions

1. Cut the cauliflower into florets, wash them well, and dry thoroughly.
2. Place the florets on a baking tray. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt.
3. Roast at 180°C for 20–25 minutes, until tender and lightly golden.
4. Meanwhile, heat 3 tbsp olive oil in a pan. Add the crushed garlic and sauté until fragrant.
5. Add the chopped coriander and piment d'Espelette. Stir well and cook for a minute or two.
6. Once the cauliflower is cooked, add it to the pan and toss well with the coriander and garlic mixture.
7. Finish with a squeeze of fresh lemon juice, toss once more, and enjoy!
