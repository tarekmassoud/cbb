## Ingredients

- 2 medium courgettes (zucchini), thinly sliced into rounds
- 200 g mozzarella, grated
- 3–4 tbsp pesto
- 150–200 g grilled halloumi, sliced into thin strips
- 1 cucumber, thinly sliced into strips
- 1–2 tomatoes, deseeded and thinly sliced
- 1 green pepper, thinly sliced

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
2. Line a baking tray with baking paper and sprinkle a thin layer of grated mozzarella over the surface.
3. Arrange the courgette slices in overlapping rows on top of the mozzarella, covering the entire tray.
4. Sprinkle the remaining mozzarella over the courgettes.
5. Bake for 20 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and the courgette base is lightly golden.
6. Remove from the oven and allow the courgette base to cool completely.
7. Spread the pesto evenly over the cooled courgette layer.
8. Arrange the grilled halloumi, cucumber, tomatoes, and green pepper evenly over the surface.
9. Starting from one end, carefully roll the roulade tightly.
10. Chill for 15–30 minutes if desired, then slice into rounds and serve.
