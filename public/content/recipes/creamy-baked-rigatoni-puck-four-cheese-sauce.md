## Ingredients

- 400 g rigatoni
- 1 aubergine
- 2 medium tomatoes
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 brick Puck Lebanon Four Cheese Sauce
- Mozzarella, grated
- Black pepper
- Salt, for the pasta water

## Instructions

1. Place the aubergine, tomatoes, and garlic in a pot. Cover with a lid and let them cook over low to medium heat in their own steam until completely tender. Do not add water.
2. Remove from the heat and peel the vegetables while they are still warm.
3. Using an immersion blender, blend the vegetables until smooth.
4. Add the brick of Puck Lebanon Four Cheese Sauce and mix until the sauce is smooth and creamy.
5. Meanwhile, cook the rigatoni in salted boiling water until al dente. Drain well.
6. Add the cooked rigatoni to the sauce and mix until every piece is well coated.
7. Transfer to an ovenproof dish, sprinkle with grated mozzarella and a little black pepper.
8. Bake for about 5 minutes, or until the mozzarella has melted.
9. Serve hot and enjoy.
