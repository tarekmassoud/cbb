## Ingredients

### For the chicken wings
- 1 kg chicken wings, cleaned and thoroughly dried
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 1/2 tbsp paprika
- 1 tsp black pepper
- Salt, to taste

### For the creamy sauce
- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp sour cream
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Fresh chives, finely chopped
- Salt, to taste

## Instructions

1. Dry the chicken wings very well with paper towels.
2. Toss the wings with baking powder, garlic powder, paprika, black pepper, and salt until evenly coated.
3. Arrange on a baking rack in a single layer and bake at 200°C for about 40–45 minutes, until golden and crispy, turning halfway through.
4. For the sauce, mix the Greek yogurt, sour cream, Worcestershire sauce, white pepper, garlic powder, chives, and salt until smooth.
5. Serve the wings hot with the creamy sauce on the side.
