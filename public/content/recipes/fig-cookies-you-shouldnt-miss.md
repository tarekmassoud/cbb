## Ingredients

- 100 g unsalted butter, softened
- 100 g soft light brown sugar
- 100 g plain flour
- 100 g rolled oats
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 1 tbsp whole milk
- 8 black figs, halved lengthwise

## Instructions

1. In a food processor, mix the softened butter and brown sugar until light and fluffy.
2. Add the flour, oats, baking powder, baking soda, and milk. Process until well combined and a soft dough forms.
3. Divide the dough into 6 equal portions and shape each into a ball. Flatten each ball slightly and place on a lined baking tray.
4. Cut each fig half into 4 wedges, leaving the wedges attached at the base.
5. Place one fig half on each cookie and gently press it into the dough.
6. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C for 12–15 minutes, or until the cookie edges and fig wedges are nicely browned.
7. Let cool slightly and enjoy.
