## Ingredients

- Lebanese black figs
- Peanut butter
- Dark chocolate
- A little coconut oil
- Toasted chopped almonds

## Instructions

1. Cut a small piece from the top and bottom of each fig. Place the figs on a tray and gently squeeze them open.
2. Spread peanut butter over each fig.
3. Melt the dark chocolate with a little coconut oil using a bain-marie.
4. Toast the chopped almonds lightly in a dry pan until golden.
5. Pour the melted chocolate over the peanut butter, then sprinkle with the toasted chopped almonds.
6. Place in the freezer for 30 minutes to 1 hour.
