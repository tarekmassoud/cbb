## Ingredients

- 3 cups flour (about 135 g per cup)
- 1/2 cup toasted sesame seeds
- 1 handful fresh green Lebanese za’atar, chopped
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 3/4 cup olive oil
- 3/4 cup water, plus a bit more if needed
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
2. In a bowl, combine the flour, sesame seeds, chopped fresh green za’atar, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
3. Add the olive oil and water. Mix until a smooth dough forms.
4. Roll the dough out thinly between two sheets of parchment paper.
5. Cut into squares, rectangles, or any desired shape.
6. Arrange on a baking tray.
7. Bake for about 30 minutes, or until golden and crisp.
8. Allow to cool completely before storing in an airtight container.
9. Serve as a crunchy snack or alongside cheese, labneh, or dips.
