## Ingredients

### For the Grilled Calamari

- 1 kg cleaned calamari tubes and tentacles
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Salt and pepper

### For the Grilled Shrimp

- 1 kg large shrimp, peeled and deveined (tails on if desired)
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- Zest of 1 lime or lemon
- 1 tbsp lime or lemon juice
- Salt and black pepper

### For the Tropical Lime Dressing

- 3 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp honey (optional)
- 1 small garlic clove, very finely grated
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### To Serve

- Crisp lettuce leaves

## Instructions

### Grilled Calamari

1. Pat the calamari very dry with paper towels.
2. Score the inside of the tubes in a crisscross pattern, if desired.
3. Toss with olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
4. Marinate for 15 to 20 minutes only.
5. Heat a grill or a plancha until very hot.
6. Grill the tubes for 1 1/2 to 2 minutes per side and the tentacles for 2 to 3 minutes total.
7. Remove immediately when opaque and lightly charred.

### Grilled Shrimp

1. Pat the shrimp dry.
2. Toss with olive oil, garlic, citrus zest, juice, salt, and pepper.
3. Marinate for 15 to 20 minutes.
4. Preheat the grill or grill pan to high heat.
5. Grill for 1 1/2 to 2 minutes per side, depending on size.

### Tropical Lime Dressing

1. Whisk together the lime juice, lemon juice, olive oil, honey, grated garlic, Dijon mustard, salt, and black pepper.

### Serve

1. Arrange crisp lettuce on a platter.
2. Top with the grilled calamari and shrimp.
3. Drizzle with the dressing and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Calamari is either cooked very quickly (2 to 4 minutes) or very slowly (45+ minutes). Anything in between tends to become rubbery.
- Shrimp are ready when they turn pink and opaque and curl into a loose “C” shape.
