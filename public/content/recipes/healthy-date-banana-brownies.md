## Ingredients

- 10 soft fresh dates (200 g), pitted
- 1 cup (260 g) mashed ripe banana (about 2–3 bananas)
- 2/3 cup (70 g) cocoa powder
- 1 1/2 cups (180 g) almond meal (ground almonds)
- Brown sugar, to taste (0–1/2 cup / 0–120 g)
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) light-flavored extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) pure honey

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 160°C (325°F). Line a 20 cm (8-inch) square baking tin with parchment paper.
2. Place the dates, mashed banana, cocoa powder, almond meal, olive oil, vanilla extract, and honey into a food processor. Blend until smooth.
3. Taste the batter before adding any sugar. You can leave it naturally sweet, add 1/4 cup (60 g) of brown sugar for a lightly sweet brownie, or add 1/2 cup (120 g) for a sweeter result. Blend again briefly to combine if you add sugar.
4. Pour the batter into the prepared tin, scraping down the sides of the processor with a spatula if needed. Smooth the top.
5. Bake for 30 minutes, or until the edges are set and the center is just firm.
6. Allow the brownies to cool in the tin for 20 minutes, then transfer them to the refrigerator until completely chilled.
7. Lift the brownie out of the tin, cut into squares with a sharp knife, and serve.
8. Store the brownies in an airtight container in the refrigerator.
