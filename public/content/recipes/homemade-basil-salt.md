## Ingredients

- 1 cup coarse salt (gros sel)
- 2 cups fresh basil leaves, washed & completely dried

## Instructions

1. Blend the salt and basil in a food processor until finely combined.
2. Spread on a baking tray and dry in a 200°F (95°C) oven for 10–15 minutes.
3. Let it cool completely, then store in a jar and use to add a delicious basil flavor to your dishes.
