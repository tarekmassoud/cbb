## Ingredients

- 300 g fresh chili peppers
- 20 g salt (approximately 3 tsp)
- 3–4 garlic cloves
- 1/4 cup olive oil, for cooking
- A little extra olive oil, for blending
- 1 tsp white wine vinegar

## Instructions

1. Wash the chili peppers thoroughly and remove the stems. Keep the seeds if you prefer a spicier purée.
2. Place the chili peppers and garlic cloves in a food processor. Add all 20 g of salt and a little olive oil, then blend until smooth.
3. Heat 1/4 cup olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
4. Add the blended chili purée to the hot olive oil and cook for about 10 minutes, until it thickens, stirring occasionally.
5. Turn off the heat and stir in the white wine vinegar.
6. Transfer the purée to a clean, sterilized jar while still hot.
7. Cover the surface completely with a layer of olive oil and seal the jar. Freezing in small portions is the safest option for keeping it longer.
