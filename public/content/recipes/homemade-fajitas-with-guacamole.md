## Ingredients

- 8 chicken breasts
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 2 red bell peppers
- 2 yellow bell peppers
- 2 green bell peppers
- 3 onions, cut into wedges
- Olive oil
- 6 ripe avocados
- Juice of 2 lemons
- Salt, to taste
- Homemade fajita breads or store-bought tortillas

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken breasts with the garlic powder, paprika, black pepper, salt, chili powder, cumin, and oregano. Mix well so every piece is evenly coated.
2. Cover and refrigerate for 2 hours to let the flavors develop.
3. Slice the bell peppers and cut the onions into wedges.
4. Drizzle with olive oil, cover, and cook over medium heat for about 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are soft and lightly caramelized.
5. Scoop the avocado flesh into a bowl and immediately add the lemon juice to prevent browning.
6. Mash well with a potato masher or a fork until smooth.
7. Season with salt and add more lemon juice to taste. Continue mashing until you have a creamy, smooth guacamole.
8. Grill the marinated chicken breasts until they are cooked through and nicely golden on both sides. Let them rest for a few minutes, then slice into long strips.
9. Heat your homemade fajita breads or store-bought tortillas until warm and soft.
10. Fill each tortilla with grilled chicken, cooked peppers and onions, and a generous spoonful of homemade guacamole.
11. Fold and serve immediately.
