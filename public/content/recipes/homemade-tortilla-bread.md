## Ingredients

- 4 cups white spelt flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 6 tbsp olive oil
- 1 1/2 cups warm water

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, combine the flour and salt.
2. Add the olive oil and warm water, then mix until a dough forms.
3. Knead for 5–7 minutes until smooth and elastic.
4. Cover and let the dough rest for 20–30 minutes.
5. Divide into equal portions, roll each into a thin circle, and cook in a hot skillet for 30–60 seconds per side, until lightly golden with brown spots.
6. Stack the tortillas and keep them covered with a clean kitchen towel to keep them soft until serving.
