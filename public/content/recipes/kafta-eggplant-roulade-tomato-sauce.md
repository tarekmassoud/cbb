## Ingredients

### For the vegetables
- 2 eggplants, sliced lengthwise
- 1 yellow bell pepper, cut into wedges
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into wedges
- 2 onions, cut into wedges
- Olive oil
- Salt

### For the kafta
- 1/2 kg (1.1 lb) lamb mince
- 1 small onion, finely diced
- 1 small bunch parsley, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1/2 tbsp black pepper

### For the tomato sauce
- 250–300 ml tomato sauce
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1/2 tsp red pepper paste (معجون فليفلة)
- Olive oil

### For serving
- Toasted pine nuts
- Chopped fresh parsley
- Steamed rice

## Instructions

1. Cut the yellow and red bell peppers and the two onions into wedges. Heat a little olive oil in a large pan and sauté them over medium-high heat until they start to soften and caramelize slightly.
2. Slice the eggplants lengthwise. Sprinkle them with salt and leave for a few minutes to release their excess water, then pat dry.
3. Arrange the eggplant slices on a baking tray, drizzle with olive oil, and roast at 180°C for 10–15 minutes, until softened and flexible enough to roll.
4. In a bowl, combine the lamb mince, finely diced onion, chopped parsley, salt, and black pepper. Mix well, then shape the kafta into large finger-shaped pieces.
5. Place the kafta pieces in the same pan with the onions and peppers and cook until lightly browned on the outside.
6. In a saucepan, combine the tomato sauce, tomato paste, and red pepper paste. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook until the sauce thickens slightly.
7. Pour the tomato sauce over the sautéed onions and peppers in an ovenproof dish.
8. Place one kafta finger on each roasted eggplant slice and roll the eggplant around the kafta to form a roulade.
9. Arrange the eggplant roulades on top of the tomato sauce and vegetables.
10. Bake uncovered at 180°C for approximately 45 minutes, until the kafta is fully cooked and the sauce is bubbling.
11. Remove from the oven and garnish with toasted pine nuts and freshly chopped parsley.
12. Serve hot with steamed rice.
