## Ingredients

### For the Cheese

- 10 liters fresh raw milk (not pasteurized and not boiled)
- 1/4 teaspoon rennet

### For the Brine

- 5 cups water
- 1 cup coarse salt

## Instructions

### Prepare the Milk

1. Warm the milk over low heat until it reaches 40°C. Do not exceed this temperature.
2. Add the rennet and mix well. Leave the milk undisturbed for 1 hour, or until it sets and becomes firm.

### Cut the Curd

1. Cut the curd lengthwise and crosswise into large cubes.
2. Let it rest for 5 minutes.
3. Using a spoon, gently stir the curds, breaking the larger cubes into smaller pieces.
4. Repeat this process 5 times, stirring every 5 minutes.

### Shape the Cheese

1. Remove the curds from the whey.
2. Take small portions in your hands and shape them into individual cheese rounds.
3. Make a small hole in the center of each cheese with your fingers and fill it with coarse salt.
4. Place the cheeses in containers and refrigerate for 48 hours.

### Drain the Cheese

1. After 48 hours, transfer the cheeses to a sieve.
2. Cover with plastic wrap and return to the refrigerator.
3. Allow the cheese to drain completely, removing as much liquid as possible.

### Prepare the Brine

1. Combine the water and coarse salt.
2. Bring to a boil, then allow the brine to cool completely.

### Store the Cheese

1. Lightly rinse the drained cheese with water.
2. Place the cheeses in a clean glass jar and cover completely with the cooled brine.
3. Seal the jar and refrigerate for 2 months.

### Serve

1. After 2 months, enjoy the homemade jibneh malha with fresh tomatoes, lettuce, olives, and warm Lebanese bread.
