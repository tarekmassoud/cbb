## Ingredients

### Brownie base
- 200 g chocolate (dark or baking chocolate)
- 200 g butter
- 4 eggs
- 250 g granulated sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 160 g flour
- 30 g cocoa powder

### Cream cheese swirl
- 8 Puck cream cheese squares
- 1 egg
- 50 g granulated sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C and line a baking pan with parchment paper.
2. Melt the chocolate and butter together in a bain-marie. Stir until smooth.
3. In a bowl, mix the eggs, granulated sugar, and vanilla extract.
4. Add the flour and cocoa powder, then mix until homogeneous. Add the melted chocolate and butter mixture and mix until well combined.
5. In a separate bowl, mix the Puck cream cheese squares, egg, granulated sugar, and vanilla extract until smooth and creamy.
6. Pour the brownie batter into the prepared pan.
7. Add spoonfuls of the cream cheese mixture over the top.
8. Use a knife or skewer to gently swirl the cream cheese into the brownie batter, creating a marbled effect.
9. Bake at 175°C for approximately 30–35 minutes, or until the edges are set and the center is still slightly soft.
10. Let the brownies cool completely before cutting into squares. Keep in the fridge in an airtight container for up to 10 days.
