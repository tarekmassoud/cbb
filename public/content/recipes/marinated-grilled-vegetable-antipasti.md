## Ingredients

- 2 aubergines, sliced lengthways
- 4 courgettes, sliced lengthways
- 1 yellow pepper, quartered
- 250 g button chestnut mushrooms
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 2 tsp oregano leaves
- Olive oil, for brushing
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat a griddle pan over high heat. Brush the aubergines generously with olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and grill until tender and beautifully charred. Transfer to a wide, shallow bowl and cover with cling film while still warm — this allows the steam to soften the vegetables and helps them absorb the flavours.
2. Grill the courgettes and yellow pepper in the same way, adding them to the bowl as they are ready and covering the bowl again with cling film.
3. Meanwhile, heat a little olive oil in a frying pan and cook the mushrooms with some seasoning until golden and lightly browned. Add them to the bowl with the other vegetables.
4. Pour over the red wine vinegar and sprinkle with the oregano.
5. Gently toss everything together, cover the bowl with cling film, and leave to marinate for about 1 hour.
6. Arrange the vegetables on a serving platter, spoon over some of the juices from the bowl, and serve at room temperature.
