## Ingredients

- 1.5–2 kg small ara'a (squash), washed and cored
- 500–700 g small eggplants, washed and cored
- 2 cups short- or medium-grain rice, rinsed and drained
- 1 1/2 cups dried chickpeas, soaked overnight, drained and peeled
- 2 medium tomatoes, finely chopped
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 1/2 tsp salt, or to taste
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2+ tbsp sumac
- 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
- 350 ml tomato juice mixed with 1/2 cup water
- Extra salt, lemon juice, and olive oil for the sauce, to taste

## Instructions

1. Soak the chickpeas overnight. Drain, peel, and set aside. Do not cook them.
2. Core the ara'a and eggplants, leaving enough flesh so they remain intact.
3. Mix the rice, chickpeas, tomatoes, onion, parsley, olive oil, salt, pepper, sumac, and lemon juice.
4. Stuff the vegetables, leaving some space at the top for the rice and chickpeas to expand.
5. Arrange them tightly in a large pot.
6. Mix some of the leftover filling with the tomato juice and water. Season with salt, lemon juice, and olive oil.
7. Pour the sauce into the pot, around and over the vegetables.
8. Cover and bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
9. Reduce the heat and simmer gently for about 40 minutes, until the rice and chickpeas are cooked and the vegetables are tender.
10. Turn off the heat and let the mehshi rest, covered, for about 40 minutes.
11. Serve cold with plenty of the flavorful tomato sauce.
