## Ingredients

- 1 can fasulya (white beans), already cooked and drained, or homemade cooked beans
- Olive oil
- Balsamic vinegar
- A little piment d'Espelette
- Salt, to taste
- Black pepper, to taste
- A little chopped parsley
- 1/2 small red onion, finely chopped
- 1 tomato, chopped
- 3–4 sun-dried tomatoes, finely chopped
- A little shoummar (fennel fronds), finely chopped
- A squeeze of lemon

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, start by adding a generous drizzle of olive oil and a little balsamic vinegar.
2. Season with a little piment d'Espelette, salt, and black pepper.
3. Add the chopped parsley, red onion, fresh tomato, sun-dried tomatoes, and a little chopped shoummar.
4. Add the cooked fasulya and mix well until everything is beautifully combined.
5. Finish with a squeeze of lemon, taste, and adjust the seasoning if needed.
6. Serve and enjoy this delicious new way of eating fasulya mtabbalé.
