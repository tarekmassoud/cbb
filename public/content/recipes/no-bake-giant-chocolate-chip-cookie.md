## Ingredients

### For the Cookie

- 100 g ground almonds (almond flour)
- 100 g oat flour
- 30 g peanut butter
- 30 g honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Chocolate chunks or chips

### For the Topping

- 100 g dark chocolate, melted
- A pinch of sea salt (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, combine the almond flour and oat flour.
2. Add the peanut butter, honey, and vanilla extract.
3. Mix until a soft dough forms.
4. Fold in the chocolate chips.
5. Press the mixture into a round cake tin, tart tin, or shallow dish, smoothing the surface with the back of a spoon.
6. Melt the dark chocolate and spread it evenly over the top.
7. Sprinkle with a pinch of sea salt.
8. Place in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours, or until firm.
9. Slice and serve.
