## Ingredients

### Vanilla
- 2 cups Puck Whipping Cream
- 397 g sweetened condensed milk
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 tsp bourbon (optional)
- Rainbow sprinkles (optional)

### Chocolate
- 2 cups Puck Whipping Cream
- 397 g sweetened condensed milk
- 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- Pinch of salt
- Crushed Oreo cookies (optional)

### Mixed Berry
- 2 1/2 cups Puck Whipping Cream
- 397 g sweetened condensed milk
- 450 g frozen mixed berries
- Zest of 1/2 lemon
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- Fresh blueberries, for serving

## Instructions

1. Chill your mixing bowl.
2. Use cold Puck Whipping Cream straight from the refrigerator. Whip until soft peaks form, about 7 minutes.
3. In another bowl, combine the sweetened condensed milk with your chosen flavor ingredients.
4. Gently fold the condensed milk mixture into the whipped cream.
5. Transfer to a freezer-safe container and freeze for at least 5 hours.
