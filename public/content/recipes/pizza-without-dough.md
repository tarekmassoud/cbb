## Ingredients

### For the base
- 1 cup ricotta or cottage cheese
- 4 eggs
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp dried oregano
- A pinch of black pepper

### For the topping
- Pizza sauce
- Jambon, salami, or any topping of your choice
- Mozzarella cheese
- 1 black olive per pizza

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, mix the ricotta or cottage cheese, eggs, olive oil, salt, garlic powder, oregano, and black pepper until well combined.
2. Pour the mixture into a greased muffin tin, filling each cup.
3. Bake in a preheated 190°C oven for about 12–15 minutes, until the bases are lightly cooked and set.
4. Remove from the oven and top each one with pizza sauce, jambon or salami, and mozzarella cheese. Add one black olive on top.
5. Return to the oven and bake for another 8–10 minutes, until the mozzarella is melted and golden.
6. Let them cool slightly, then enjoy your delicious pizza without dough!
