## Ingredients

- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 3 eggs
- 200 ml olive oil, plus 1 tbsp for sautéing
- 250 ml whole milk
- 400 g self-raising flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 1/2 tsp caster sugar
- 165 g pitted black olives
- 200 g halloumi, cut into 2 1/2 cm cubes
- 50 g halloumi, finely grated, divided
- 4 tbsp fresh oregano, finely chopped
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 tsp nigella seeds
- Pumpkin seeds, for topping

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan / 180°C gas. Grease and line a loaf tin with baking paper.
2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a pan and gently sauté the onion until soft. Set aside to cool.
3. In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, and 200 ml olive oil.
4. Add the self-raising flour, baking powder, and caster sugar. Mix gently until combined.
5. Fold in the cooked onion, black olives, cubed halloumi, 25 g of the finely grated halloumi, and oregano.
6. Pour the mixture into the prepared tin and smooth the top.
7. Sprinkle the remaining 25 g grated halloumi, sesame seeds, nigella seeds, and pumpkin seeds over the top.
8. Bake for approximately 45–50 minutes, or until golden and a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean.
9. Leave to cool slightly in the tin before slicing and serving.
