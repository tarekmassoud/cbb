## Ingredients

- 1 2/3 cups water
- 1 cup white quinoa (or quinoa of your choice), rinsed and drained
- 2 cups frozen chopped kale or spinach
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp soy sauce

## Instructions

1. Bring the water to a boil in a saucepan. Add the quinoa and salt, reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for about 12 minutes, or until the water is absorbed.
2. Stir in the frozen kale or spinach during the last 2–3 minutes of cooking, until wilted and heated through.
3. Remove from the heat and fluff with a fork.
4. Stir in the toasted sesame seeds, rice vinegar, toasted sesame oil, and soy sauce.
5. Serve warm alongside a fried egg, meat, or chicken.
