## Ingredients

- 2 packs ready-made shish barak
- 1 pack ready-made kibbeh
- 2 packs goat yogurt
- 1 tbsp cornflour (cornstarch)
- 1/2 cup water
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried mint, plus extra for garnish
- Roasted pine nuts, for serving
- Steamed white rice, for serving

## Instructions

1. Strain the goat yogurt through a fine sieve into a large pot until smooth.
2. Dissolve the cornflour in 1/2 cup water, then whisk it into the yogurt.
3. Place the pot over medium-high heat, stirring continuously until the yogurt comes to a gentle boil.
4. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a small pan. Sauté the crushed garlic until fragrant, then stir in the dried mint. Add this mixture to the yogurt.
5. Arrange the shish barak and kibbeh on a baking tray. Drizzle with a little olive oil and bake for about 10 minutes, or until lightly golden — since they are already cooked, this step simply crisps them up.
6. Gently add the baked shish barak and kibbeh to the hot yogurt.
7. Serve immediately with steamed white rice, garnished with roasted pine nuts and a sprinkle of dried mint.
