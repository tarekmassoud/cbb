## Ingredients

- 2 medium courgettes (zucchini), thinly sliced into rounds
- 200 g mozzarella, grated
- 200 g cream cheese
- Black pepper
- 2 ripe avocados, thinly sliced
- 150 to 200 g smoked salmon

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
2. Line a baking tray with baking paper and sprinkle a thin layer of the grated mozzarella over the surface.
3. Arrange the courgette slices in overlapping rows on top of the mozzarella, covering the entire tray.
4. Sprinkle the remaining mozzarella over the courgettes.
5. Bake for 20 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and lightly golden.
6. Remove from the oven and allow the courgette base to cool completely.
7. Mix the cream cheese with a bit of black pepper, then spread evenly over the cooled courgette layer.
8. Top with the avocado, then arrange the smoked salmon evenly over the surface.
9. Starting from one end, carefully roll the roulade tightly.
10. Chill for 15 to 30 minutes if desired, then slice into rounds and serve.
