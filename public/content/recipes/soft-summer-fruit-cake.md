## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 1/3 cup coconut oil
- 1 cup plain natural yogurt
- 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup desiccated coconut
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 260 g all-purpose flour
- Some pitted cherries and two halved peaches
- Extra sugar, for sprinkling (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and grease or line a cake pan.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs, vegetable oil, yogurt, lemon zest, and brown sugar until smooth.
3. Add the desiccated coconut, flour, and baking powder, then gently mix until just combined.
4. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and arrange the cherry halves over the top, pressing them in slightly.
5. Sprinkle a little extra sugar over the surface, if desired.
6. Bake for 45 minutes, or until a skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
7. Allow the cake to cool before slicing and serving.
