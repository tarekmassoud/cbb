## Ingredients

- 350 g white spelt flour
- 100 ml extra-virgin olive oil
- 110–115 ml dry white wine (start with 110 ml and add up to 5 ml more if the dough feels too dry)
- 8–9 g fine salt

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine the flour and salt.
2. Add the olive oil and white wine, then mix until a smooth dough forms.
3. Knead for a few minutes until soft and elastic.
4. Cover and let the dough rest for 15–20 minutes.
5. Divide the dough into small pieces, roll each into a rope, and join the ends to form rings.
6. Bring a large pot of water to a gentle boil.
7. Boil the taralli in batches until they float to the surface, then remove with a slotted spoon and place on a clean towel or baking paper to dry.
8. Arrange on a baking tray lined with parchment paper.
9. Bake in a preheated oven at 190°C (375°F) for 25–35 minutes, or until golden and crisp.
10. Let the taralli cool completely before serving—they become even crunchier as they cool.
