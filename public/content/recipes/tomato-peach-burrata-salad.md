## Ingredients

- 2 large heirloom tomatoes, sliced
- 2–3 ripe peaches, sliced
- 1 1/2 cups grape or cherry tomatoes, halved
- 10 to 12 pitted cherries
- 8 oz x 2 burrata cheese, drained and patted dry
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- Olive oil, for drizzling
- Balsamic vinegar, for drizzling
- Fresh basil, chopped, for garnish
- Grilled bread, for serving

## Instructions

1. Arrange the sliced heirloom tomatoes, peaches, cherries, and cherry tomatoes on a large serving platter.
2. Add the basil leaves.
3. Tear the burrata into pieces and scatter it over the tomatoes and peaches.
4. Season everything with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
5. Drizzle with the olive oil and the balsamic vinegar.
6. Serve immediately with grilled bread, if desired.
