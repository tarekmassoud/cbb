## Ingredients

- 1/4 watermelon, cut into small triangles
- 3 halloumi cheese, cut into sticks
- 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- A handful of mint leaves

## Instructions

1. Cut the watermelon into small triangles and arrange on a serving plate.
2. Grill the halloumi sticks in a hot pan for 1–2 minutes per side until golden and lightly charred.
3. Scatter the grilled halloumi over the watermelon.
4. Drizzle with the white wine vinegar and olive oil.
5. Top with fresh mint leaves and serve immediately.
