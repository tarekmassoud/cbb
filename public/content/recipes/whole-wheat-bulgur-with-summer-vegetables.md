## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large red onion, diced
- 1 large yellow courgette (yellow zucchini), cut into large cubes
- 1 large eggplant, cut into large cubes
- 2 bell peppers, cut into large pieces
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- 1 tsp chili paste
- 250 g homemade tomato sauce
- 1 1/2 cups whole wheat coarse bulgur
- 3 cups water

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add the diced red onion and sauté until softened.
3. Add the courgette, eggplant, and bell peppers. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables begin to soften and lightly brown.
4. Season with salt and black pepper, then stir in the chili paste.
5. Add the homemade tomato sauce and cook for 2 to 3 minutes.
6. Stir in the whole wheat coarse bulgur, then pour in the water and mix well.
7. Bring to a boil, then cover the pot, reduce the heat to medium-low, and cook for about 15 to 20 minutes, or until the bulgur has absorbed all the liquid and is tender.
8. Fluff with a fork before serving.
