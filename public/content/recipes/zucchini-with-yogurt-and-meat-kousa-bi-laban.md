## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 kg zucchini (kousa), cut into rounds
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 large onion, diced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 300 g lamb or beef meat, cut into small cubes
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 kg plain yogurt
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp dried mint, crushed
- Toasted pine nuts
- Additional dried mint
- Steamed rice

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
2. Place the zucchini rounds on a baking tray.
3. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper.
4. Roast for about 20 minutes, or until tender and lightly golden.
5. Heat the olive oil in a large pan.
6. Add the diced onion and sauté until translucent.
7. Add the meat, cook until the meat is fully cooked and lightly browned, and season with salt and pepper.
8. Add the roasted zucchini to the pan and mix gently.
9. Place the yogurt in a pot over medium-high heat.
10. Stir continuously until it comes to a boil.
11. In a small pan, heat the olive oil and sauté the crushed garlic until fragrant.
12. Add the crushed dried mint and stir for a few seconds.
13. Add the garlic and mint mixture to the boiling yogurt and mix well.
14. Pour the yogurt sauce over the meat and zucchini mixture.
15. Let everything simmer together for a few minutes.
16. Sprinkle with toasted pine nuts and extra dried mint.
17. Serve hot with steamed rice.
